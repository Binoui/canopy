# Trees

Ctrl+E and Forest open Trees. Tabs appear in stored tab order, pinned first. Saved for later is a separate shelf, with the most recently saved tree first. A tree appears in exactly one place.

Click or Enter on a card resumes its last page and closes the forest. Alt+Enter opens its tab without changing the page you are reading. Menus offer Pin tab or Unpin tab, Save for later, Rename and Settle tree on Tabs. The shelf offers Open, Rename and Settle tree. Mutations keep the forest open.

The tab bar, overflow list and Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+9 follow stored order. New and reopened trees append to Tabs. Unpin leaves a tab at the border after pinned tabs. Ctrl+Shift+Tab still returns to the previously shown tab. Drag tabs in the bar to reorder them, including pinned icon tabs. Dropping among pinned tabs pins the source; dropping among other tabs unpins it. A drop at their border keeps its pin. Ctrl+Shift+PageUp and Ctrl+Shift+PageDown move the active tab within its group. Alt+Up and Alt+Down on a forest card use the same movement rule.
