# Canopy

Press **Ctrl+E** or click **Canopy** at the left of the tabs to open the map. It starts at Middle with the current page in view. The current tree comes first, followed by the other tabs in their stored order. Each tree has its name and page count. Saved and settled trees remain available on the new-tree page and in History.

**You are here** marks the current page. **Back** marks the last other page shown in this tree. The header's **Back to** button returns to that page and closes the map. It can alternate between a conversation and a test opened several pages deep. Returning through a folded site mark opens its most recently shown page, or its first page if none has been shown.

The map distinguishes pages still open, visited pages whose view was released, and waiting pages never opened. A waiting page stays waiting after loading in the background. A page grows with its new and return showings, slowly and up to a cap. Reloads affect recency but do not make it bigger. Rest the pointer on an item for its full title, site, state and showing count.

Use the wheel, **+**, **-**, or **Far**, **Middle** and **Near** to zoom. The point of the page under the pointer stays in place when a site unfolds or folds. Drag to move the map. A drag does not open a page.

- **Far** folds every connected run on one site into an icon and page count. Both current and previous marks remain visible. Only You are here has a text tag.
- **Middle** keeps runs folded and names the current page, the previous page and pages shown at least three times. Runs show their site and page count.
- **Near** shows every drawn page with its title, site and state.

Flow runs left to right. Far and Middle arrange trees beside and below one another. Near stacks trees. A tree too large to fit can be panned. Put-away branches and dismissed waiting pages stay off the map.

Click a page, a folded mark or a tree name to go there and close Canopy. A tree name resumes its last page. Arrow keys move focus in the requested direction. **Enter** opens the focused item. **Tab** stays in the pop-in. **Escape** closes it. Right-click, **Menu** or **Shift+F10** opens the shared page or tree menu. Actions that do not navigate keep Canopy open.

**Ctrl+H** switches to History. **Ctrl+E** from History switches to Canopy. Pressing the shortcut for the view already showing closes it.

Flow is the only layout in this version. Islands, a stored layout choice and touchpad pinch belong to the next change.
